# Day 3

Decided to stop taking notes in a random notes app and actually build something to hold them.

## What I learned

- `import.meta.glob` can load markdown files as raw text at build time — no backend needed.
- Keeping frontmatter parsing tiny (no extra dependency) is enough for three fields: date, title, description.

## What I built

The first version of this timeline — desktop drawer, mobile bottom sheet, and this very entry rendering inside it.

## What I struggled with

Getting the bottom sheet to feel "natural" on touch instead of just being a drawer that opens from a different side.
