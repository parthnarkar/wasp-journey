# Day 1

Today was mostly reading. I wanted to actually understand how Wasp turns a spec file into a running app instead of just copy-pasting from the tutorial.

## What I learned

- The `main.wasp.ts` file isn't config in the usual sense — it's a spec the compiler reads to generate glue code.
- Pages and routes are separate concepts. A page owns the component, a route owns the path.
- Queries and actions are just typed RPC functions Wasp wires up for you.

Nothing shipped today. I sketched a diagram of how a page connects to a route in my notebook.
